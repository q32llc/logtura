# What Logtura sees

Logtura is comfortable storing control-plane metadata and operational health signals. Logtura should not become your log warehouse or a proxy for your production log streams.

## We are okay with this

- Account and connection metadata
- Encrypted provider and deploy credentials
- Discovered source names, IDs, and hierarchy
- Deployment status and generated config metadata
- Heartbeats, event counts, rates, and health metrics

## We do not want this

- Stored application log content
- A central Logtura-hosted log tailing fleet
- Byte-metered custody of your production logs
- Provider streams proxied through our infrastructure
- Any architecture that makes us your log warehouse

## Why we do not run collectors for you

Running collectors on Logtura infrastructure would make the product simpler to explain, but it would be the wrong boundary.

Keeping the forwarder in your infrastructure gives you clearer data custody, lower blast radius, more predictable costs, and an escape hatch. If Logtura disappears, your open-source forwarder can keep running.
